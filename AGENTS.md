# Alloy

> an engine that lets you build an intelligent system that is distrubuted and evolves with you over time

### core goals:

- give your intelligence a free space to evolve and adapt to your ways
- making personal intelligence self owned and distributed
- provide a way to curb redundance in setups and deployement
- super fast and secure transfer of information
- language, runtime, os, provider and harness agnostic

### non goals:

- not replace current agentic paradigms and toolings but complement them
- not replace interfaces or fight the ai interfacing products and conventions
- not impose bias on how personal intelligent systems should grow or behave

### thinking principles

- ai doesn't have to store what it learned about you in only text, vectors and graphs; written software and code can provide a more sophisticate way to store it's learning about you and adaptation of your habits
- we create a space for this learnings to grow and execute in distributes space with access to tools necessary to execute, store and communicate these learnings
